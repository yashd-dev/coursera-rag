A.4. Configurations, matching, and reuse
Each configuration in Tables 2 and 4 is trained independently from its corresponding saved
initialization and uses its assigned readout throughout training and evaluation.
The pointer and mean-span heads have identical parameter counts: 1,311,232 parameters.
All-module LoRA adds 129,859,584 parameters; attention and DeltaNet adapters alone add
57,507,840. The auxiliary all-module rank-28 control uses 𝛼 = 56 and totals 58,124,800 trainable
parameters, keeping 𝛼/𝑟LoRA = 2. Its adapter shapes differ from rank 64.
Within each fold–seed setting, the core learning-rate models start from common saved
parameters. The adapter-scope and rank comparisons copy reference parameters wherever
their dimensions agree and align the initial CPU/CUDA random-generator states. Separate
generators control record shuffling and augmentation, with matching recorded orders, inputs,
and generator traces across the comparisons. A repeated pointer executes the reference recipe
again under the same recorded starting conditions.

and frozen-branch controls across four settings gives 28 models. The adapter-scope and rank
comparisons add 20 models while reusing the four reference pointers; eight two-epoch models
then reuse those pointers and the four all-module pooling models as their one-epoch references,
giving 56 distinct trainings. The joint learning-rate experiments on H100, the additional adapterscope comparison, and the Qwen3 experiments each use their own references.
