Evaluation selects the first maximal candidate in the recorded order and requires three finite

revisions, splits, and row order for the evaluated populations.
A.2. Training corpus and grouped folds
The predominantly English corpus contains 22,035 records and 29,897 questions drawn from web
decisions, policy tasks, and public classification datasets. It combines 4,642 JevForge/Mind2Web
records [12], 2,676 Nimble records [4], 1,680 compositional-policy records, ten public-source

groups of 1,000 records each, and generated policy, date, insufficient-evidence, control, and
assertion records. The ten public sources are Yelp full-star reviews, DBpedia14, and AG News
[52]; Banking77 [7]; TREC [23]; MultiNLI [46]; IMDb [28]; sentence-level SST-5 [38]; BoolQ [9];
and the SetFit English packaging of MARC [19], with all source labels converted to the shared
decision schema.
JevForge uses the cleaned Mind2Web resource at revision 080a4b67 and the JevForge
conversion at revision 8c6fa6c9. The conversion preserves labels and distributions, including probability mass shared by several positive candidates. Its website partition differs from
the official Mind2Web out-of-distribution split. The retained Nimble reference is revision
f136b3f75721fda4ea961f73993cc50b08488835. Its publisher describes the reference answers as synthetic and model-checked. Training consumes record contents directly without
opening external private-file or image references.
To keep related examples in the same split, we link records through shared families, paired or
control examples, upstream rows, websites, and normalized states, then assign each connected
group as a whole. This produces 12,472 groups, allocated with approximate feature balance:
80% to eight training pools, 5% to locked development, 5% to calibration, and 10% to a separate
test split. The eight pools contain 17,661 records and 24,001 questions, with fold 𝑗 holding out
pool 𝑗 and training on the remaining seven. Our four settings combine folds 2 and 3 with seeds
44 and 45: fold 2 contains 15,456 training records and requires 1,932 updates per epoch, while
fold 3 contains 15,442 records and requires 1,931 updates.
The corpus contains 12,486 Choice, 12,176 binary, and 5,235 Score questions. Most supplied
target distributions are one-hot (9,142 of 9,457), while the remaining distributions express
insufficient evidence through 173 uniform targets or multiple valid solutions through 142
JevForge targets. Training preserves these distributions and augments compatible requests
by shuffling options or adding distractor and none-of-the-above options, with augmentation
probabilities ranging from 0.10 to 0.25.
