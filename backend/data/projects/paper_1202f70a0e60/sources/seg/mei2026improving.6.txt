5.5. Task-Level Sources of Improvement
HellaSwag and synthetic chess account for most of the net MC11 gain (Figure 3). On HellaSwag,
pooling adds about 4.2 pp under either adapter scope, while removing FFN adapters adds about
9.1 pp with either readout, giving the final model an advantage of about 13.3 pp. On chess, the
pooling gain grows from 1.95 pp under all-module adaptation to 7.35 pp without FFN adapters.
The final model also improves both ARC configurations, both MMLU configurations, and
WinoGrande, alongside declines on GSM8K and GPQA. Appendix B gives the component contrasts for every task. The mean pooling and scope gains remain positive under both weighting
schemes when any one source is omitted (Appendix D).
