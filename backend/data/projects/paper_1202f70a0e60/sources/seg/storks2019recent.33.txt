Lexical resources. A widely used lexical resource for commonly used nouns, verbs, adjectives
and adverbs is WordNet 12 (Miller, 1995). Different from a traditional online dictionary, WordNet organizes words in terms of concepts (i.e., a list of synonyms) and their semantic relations
to other words (e.g., antonymy, hyponymy/hypernymy, entailment, etc.). There are also resources
specifically for verbs. VerbNet 13 by Schuler (2005) is a hierarchical English verb lexicon that is
created based on the verb classes from the English Verb Classes and Alternations (EVCA) resource
by Levin (1993). VerbNet defines many classes of verbs and their argument structures, selectional
restrictions on the arguments, and syntactic descriptions. Other resources for verbs include VerbOcean 14 (Chklovski & Pantel, 2004) which captures a network of finer-grained relations among a
smaller set of common verbs, and VerbCorner 15 (Hartshorne et al., 2013) which provides crowdsourced validation for VerbNet.
