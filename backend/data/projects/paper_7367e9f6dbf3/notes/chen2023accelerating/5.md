**Problem**
Targets accelerating LLM decoding via speculative sampling using Chinchilla-sized models.

**Method**
Propose training wider models with fewer layers to minimize communication overhead while maintaining performance, serving draft models on same hardware as target. Evaluate speculative sampling with Chinchilla on XSum (1-shot) and HumanEval (100-shot).

**Reported results**
- HumanEval: near 2.5× speedup.
- XSum with greedy sampling: 0.6–0.9× speedup (regression at higher K).
- Acceptance rates higher for HumanEval due to common sub-sequences and temperature sharpening.
- 7B model on 16 TPUs increases latency; optimal topology for 7B is 4 TPUs (5ms/token).

**Limitations**
- Numerics differences between ArS and SpS prevent identical outputs.
- Speedup plateaus/regresses as draft tokens (K) increase.
- XSum shows lower speedup than HumanEval.

**Two short exact quotes**
- "a chinchilla-optimal 7B achieves its lowest sampling latency on 4 TPU v4s (where it achieves 5ms/token)"
- "HumanEval reaches speedups of almost 2.5×"
